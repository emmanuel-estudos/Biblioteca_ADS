## VM1_INTERNET

Faça comexão `ssh` com a **SEED**

- ssh seed@10.3.150.90

- senha: dees

## VM2_GATEWAY

- iptables -t nat -A POSTROUTING -j MASQUERADE

- sudo su
- 654123
- route add -net 10.3.150.0 netmask 255.255.255.0 gw 192.168.56.104 (eth0 da VM2_GATEWAY)

- telnet 10.3.150.90 (IP_SEED)

- VM Login: seed
